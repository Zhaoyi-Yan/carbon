## Independent reply check

The six held layouts and 24 native team actions were chosen before considering the historical opponent's first command. The planter-stay alternative was available on one layout, Sponge v4 `79a17925` at turn 41. It recovered the logged reply's two-planter trade on one candidate, the sole case with lost planters under the historical response. The native reply predicted that neither planter would die for that candidate. The additional response did not change the selected move, and did not create a new collision on any of the 23 historical no-loss comparisons. The native sample predicted three losses on another layout where the historical reply had none. The holdout uses six layouts outside the four development maps. Some appeared at other turns in earlier experiments.

These four cases support further coverage checks in contested situations. They do not support removing the tail or retraining the critic directly. The held reply trial recovered an omitted event without demonstrating a better decision. It provides no basis for changing the deployed response mixture yet.
