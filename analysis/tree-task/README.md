# New-tree investment from ranked v8 replays

All 48 continuations reached turn 299. The A/A case reproduced the entire trajectory. The eight distinct public replay positions span turns 69–219, with three v8 seat-0 positions and five seat-1 positions. The original v8 stayed and established a new tree in each of the four historical investment positions. It moved in each of the other four. Reconstructed v8 under the fixed budget stayed in six of the sixteen proxy continuations. Both modified arms received the exact same initial joint action. After the first decision, both players reacted to the new state every turn. Local opponents were frozen v7 and v8, with one fixed random stream per proxy. Performance cannot be assigned separately to proxy identity and random stream with that design.

The first-step change was worse under both proxies on three of the eight maps. Its mean terminal changes against v7 and v8 were −188.96 and −417.60. Eight additional trees were created and none repaid the planting price from ordinary income while held by us. The average ordinary income minus planting price on those additional trees was −43.10 with the first-step-only policy and −42.59 with persistence. This accounting excludes target-specific collision payments and any indirect denial.

Persistence changed eleven trajectories. It increased the target tree's net cash contribution by 11.95 per continuation over the one-step variant, but reduced the all-team relative tree net by 22.65. Relative depot receipts rose by 108.54, accounting for most of the terminal improvement. At 18 turns it was still 48.66 below the first-step variant in total cash margin. Ten tasks exited because the planter could find no safe position, four after it was lost, and two after the initial planting failed. All of these exits count in the mean.

The exact stay edit was present in the admitted pool in six of sixteen root evaluations. In all six, v8 had already chosen to stay. In the other ten, the original pool included no joint action with that worker staying. In these positions, missing coverage of this stay action did not manifest as a repeatable winning intervention. Terminal performance stays conditional on the target eligibility rules, the local proxies and the particular defense policy, which changes actions after the inner search.

The protocol records maps, acting seats, original opponents, frozen file hashes, roots and random streams. The compressed branch archive includes both successes and failures, candidate and execution traces, ownership history totals, cash at intermediate milestones and terminal results. No online change was evaluated.
